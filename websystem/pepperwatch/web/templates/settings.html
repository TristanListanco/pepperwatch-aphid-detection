Detection Thresholds

Applied live
0.25

Lower finds more aphid damage, with more false alarms.

0.45

How much two boxes may overlap before the weaker one is suppressed. Applied per class, so an aphid cluster sitting on a leaf does not suppress the leaf.

On the AI HAT+ boxes are filtered on the chip, so a new IoU is saved now and used from the next session.

Saving Photos

Applied live

Damage appearing or clearing is always saved (at most once every 8 seconds), and so is every snapshot. Longer intervals fill the SD card more slowly.

Site & Language

Shown at the top of every page and saved with each new photo. Photos already taken keep the name they had.

Advisory text is available in English and Cebuano.

Data

Deletes every saved scan, its boxes and its images. Session CSV files (frame rates and power) are kept.

This cannot be undone. Delete all detections?

Debug Log

For finding faults

Records what the detector and the dashboard do, in full detail, until you stop it. Start it, make the fault happen, stop it, then download the log. Each log holds at most about 60 MB, and the last 5 are kept.
