0.25

Figure 3.3 of the manuscript specifies a fixed filter of 0.80. That is shipped here as an adjustable value defaulting to 0.25, because 0.80 on clusters this small discards most true detections — and Chapter IV counts missed infestations as a headline metric. Sweeping this slider and recording precision against recall is a stronger result than asserting a single number.
